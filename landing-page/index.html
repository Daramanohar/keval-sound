Features

Find it. Own it.
Shape it.

KEVAL SOUND brings AI discovery, exclusive licensing, lossless radio, premium samples, and track-editing tools into one workflow for creators, producers, and teams.

F-01 — AI MUSIC SEARCH

Describe the sound you need.

Use KEVAL SOUND’s AI search engine to find tracks by describing the mood, scene, emotion, or content style you are creating for. You can also browse by genre when you want a simpler catalog view.

F-02 — EXCLUSIVE LICENSE LOCK

Claim a track before anyone else does.

When you purchase a song, it is locked from future buyers. You receive the high-quality file, a clear license certificate, and the confidence that your chosen track will not keep circulating across other creators’ projects.

F-03 — CREATIVE TRACK TOOLS

Shape purchased songs for your project.

Go beyond downloading a track. Use tools like Get Stems and Edit Song to adapt purchased music for films, reels, ads, podcasts, games, and branded content. Pro and Enterprise users can turn licensed tracks into more flexible creative assets.
